src/alerts/: how the workflows tell the maintainer (ADR 0038). notify.ts (createNotify): one Telegram sendMessage per message, in Telegram's HTML (a bold headline with the environment, any detail lines, the paper's title in bold, a short link, every part escaped), only logged as one plain line without a bot, never throwing. messages.ts: the activity notices for a published paper, a settled rating and a delayed one (publishedNotice, ratedNotice, delayedNotice: why in plain words, Gemini's next daily reset and the request's cancel time in Paris time), with links (siteUrlOf, the site's address, which the web app's metadata uses too). on-failure.ts: the alert-on-failure function, on every inngest/function.failed and inngest/function.cancelled (a run past its time budget among them), but a rating run's delayed failure, which sent its own message. health.ts: the check-health cron, every hour, on the DKG node (its open status: answering, and the environment's graph accepted from finalized-chain), the oracle's and the publisher wallets' ETH and TRAC, and requests pending past 6 hours, reading the node in one step, the balances in another and each contract in its own, at most 2 pages of pending ids a contract an hour, with the rating folder's pending reads. Every send runs in a step of its own.
