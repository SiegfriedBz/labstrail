src/alerts/: how the workflows tell the maintainer (ADR 0038). notify.ts (createNotify): one Telegram sendMessage per message, prefixed with the environment and the level, only logged without a bot, never throwing. messages.ts: the activity texts for a published paper and a settled rating, with links (siteUrlOf). on-failure.ts: the alert-on-failure function, on every inngest/function.failed and inngest/function.cancelled (a run past its time budget among them). health.ts: the check-health cron, every hour, on the oracle's and the publisher wallets' ETH and TRAC and on requests pending past 6 hours, reading the balances in one step and each contract in its own, at most 2 pages of pending ids a contract an hour, with the rating folder's pending reads. Every send runs in a step of its own.
