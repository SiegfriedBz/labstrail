src/alerts/: how the workflows tell the maintainer (ADR 0038). notify.ts (createNotify): one Telegram sendMessage per message, in Telegram's HTML (a bold headline with the environment, the paper's title in bold, a short link, every part escaped), only logged as one plain line without a bot, never throwing. messages.ts: the activity notices for a published paper and a settled rating (publishedNotice, ratedNotice), with links (siteUrlOf). on-failure.ts: the alert-on-failure function, on every inngest/function.failed and inngest/function.cancelled (a run past its time budget among them). health.ts: the check-health cron, every hour, on the DKG node (its open status: answering, and the environment's graph accepted from finalized-chain), the oracle's and the publisher wallets' ETH and TRAC, and requests pending past 6 hours, reading the node in one step, the balances in another and each contract in its own, at most 2 pages of pending ids a contract an hour, with the rating folder's pending reads. Every send runs in a step of its own.
