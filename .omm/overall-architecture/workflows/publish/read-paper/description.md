read-paper.ts and grobid.ts: fetch the pinned PDF (src/pdf/), send it to GROBID's processHeaderDocument, read title, authors, abstract and DOI from the TEI with core's parseTeiHeader, the title completed from the PDF's stored title when GROBID dropped its first words or spaced it differently (core's completeTitle), and read the first 6,000 characters of the text with the rating's reader (paperTextOf, on a copy of the bytes, since unpdf takes over what it reads), in one step so the PDF's bytes never become a step output. GROBID's answer maps to unparseable, unauthorized or unreachable; a PDF the rating's reader cannot parse is unreadable, one with no text layer no-text.
