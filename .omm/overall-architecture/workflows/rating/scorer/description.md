scorer.ts: one Gemini request (gemini-3.8-flash, free tier) through the shared call in src/gemini/, with core's scorerPrompt at temperature 0, no thinking level (the model's default, medium) and a JSON schema; the same paper scored again can get another score, measured with score-papers --repeat (docs/domain.md → Gemini); the answer is checked with parseScorerAnswer, and scoreChecks keeps a point only when its quote is in the paper. Failures are typed (rate-limited, refused, unreachable, invalid-output) and never carry the key or Gemini's text.
