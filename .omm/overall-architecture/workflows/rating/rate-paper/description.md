rate-paper.ts: runRating and the rate-paper function (a singleton per request id, throttled outside local, a 180-minute budget). It checks the request is pending on one of our contracts, cancels invalid-target a paper verisci did not publish, then reads the R-KA's state and does only what is left: find the PDF link, read its text, score it with one Gemini request, store and mint the R-KA, read the score back, and have the oracle fulfil. Outside the field, no text, too large or three bad reads cancel invalid-target; past 24 hours, expired.
