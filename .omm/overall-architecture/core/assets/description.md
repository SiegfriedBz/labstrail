packages/core/src/assets/: what every feature uses to address things. ual.ts parses, normalizes (one canonical spelling, ADR 0031) and formats UALs; asset-names.ts names a Target KA from its PDF's CID (verisci-tka-) and an R-KA from its request id (verisci-rka-, ADR 0016); cid.ts gives a CID's canonical CIDv1 base32 spelling; asset-graph.ts gives the IRIs of a context graph and of an asset's own graph, which every SPARQL query names; chains.ts and origintrail.ts hold chain ids and OriginTrail's contract addresses, and find an asset's ERC-721 token from its UAL.
