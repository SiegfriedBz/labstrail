apps/web/app/: the pages (/, /papers, /papers/[cid], /papers/[cid]/ratings/[requestId], /publish, /method, /about), the layout with the site's metadata (siteMetadata: the site's address, the title template, each page's path as canonical, closed to crawlers outside production), header, footer and theme, globals.css (the Clinical look's tokens for both themes, held to WCAG AA contrast by globals.test.ts) and icon.svg (the mark), robots.ts and sitemap.ts (production's crawling rules and every published paper, read again hourly), opengraph-image.tsx and papers/[cid]/opengraph-image.tsx (the site's share image, and each paper's with its title, authors and score), the server actions in actions.ts (requestUpload, submitPaper, announceLocalRating), and four route handlers: /api/inngest serves the workflows' functions, /api/webhooks/alchemy hands the Alchemy Notify webhook's deliveries to the workflows' intake, and /api/papers/[cid] and /api/ratings/[requestId] give a paper's and a rating's status to the pages that poll them. The paper and rating pages answer 404 for an address that is no CID or request id before anything streams, share one read between their metadata and body, and render whole for crawlers that run no JavaScript.
