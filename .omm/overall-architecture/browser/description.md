The researcher's browser: it connects a wallet with Reown AppKit and wagmi on Base Sepolia, and locally on Anvil too (ADRs 0034, 0036), uploads a PDF straight to Pinata with a short-lived signed URL, signs the EIP-712 statement of its CID, and follows the paper's page until it is minted. From a paper's page it requests a rating, a requestPhase1 transaction its wallet pays the gas of, then follows the rating's page to its score. No session: the signature authorizes each submission.
