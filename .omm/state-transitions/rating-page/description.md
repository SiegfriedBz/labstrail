What a rating's page shows (apps/web/lib/rating/stage.ts, ratingStage, pure): the request's status on the contract and, while pending, its R-KA's state on the DKG node (no asset: scoring; draft: saving; stored: minting; minted: recording), polled through /api/ratings/[requestId] until scored or cancelled. The contract's score is the one shown (ADR 0014). With the DKG node down, the page still shows the rating from the chain, under the paper's short address, and says so (ADR 0021).
