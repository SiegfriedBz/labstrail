Step parse-paper-<n> (it replaced read-paper-<n> when its result gained the text's opening): fetches the PDF through the gateway, has GROBID read its header, and reads the start of its text with the rating's reader (workflows/src/pdf/paper-text.ts, 6,000 characters), all in workflows/src/publish/read-paper.ts. Core's parseTeiHeader gives title, authors, abstract and DOI, with accents GROBID split from their letters joined back in authors' names, and targetKaQuads the triples with the submitter's address, signature and deadline. Not a PDF, too large, unparseable, no title, a PDF the rating's reader cannot parse (unreadable) or one with no text layer (no-text) end the run refused, so every published paper can be rated.
