Step check-field-<n> (workflows/src/publish/field-check.ts, core's fieldCheckPrompt and parseFieldAnswer): asks gemini-3.5-flash-lite, on the free tier, whether a new paper is in VeriSci's field from its title, abstract and opening (ADR 0037). Outside ends the run refused: outside-field before anything is stored, logging Gemini's reason with the CID. A failed check waits 30 s (check-field-wait-<n>) and asks once more (check-field-<n>-again); a second failure publishes the paper, its rating checking the field again, and logs field-unchecked. Asked once per run: a later attempt asks nothing, and a draft or a minted asset is never checked.
