/papers/[cid]/ratings/[requestId] (app/papers/[cid]/ratings/[requestId]/page.tsx): one rating, under Papers / title / Rating. Read on the server from the environment's contracts and the DKG node, then polled through /api/ratings until final: while pending, its step chain (Scored, Saved off-chain, Minted on-chain, Recorded on-chain), when it was requested and, past 15 minutes, that it is taking long; scored, the contract's score and every check with its answer and quote; cancelled, why. A rating of another paper, or none, or any under a paper not published, is not-found, and the page checks a rating the chain shows only later; opened with ?new=1 (the Rate button), a request no contract holds yet is followed for 2 minutes, headed "Checking on this rating", before it reads "No rating here"; with the DKG node down, the rating still shows from the chain under the paper's short address, with a note (state-transitions → rating-page).
