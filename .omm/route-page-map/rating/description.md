/papers/[cid]/ratings/[requestId] (app/papers/[cid]/ratings/[requestId]/page.tsx): one rating, under Papers / title / Rating. Read on the server from the environment's contracts and the DKG node, then polled through /api/ratings until final: while pending, its step chain (Scored, Saved off-chain, Minted on-chain, Recorded on-chain), when it was requested and, past 15 minutes, that it is taking long; scored, the contract's score and every check with its answer and quote; cancelled, why. A rating of another paper, or none, is not-found; with the DKG node down, the rating still shows from the chain under the paper's short address, with a note (state-transitions → rating-page).
