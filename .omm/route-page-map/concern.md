The paper page and the papers list are rendered per request from the DKG node, so they are only as fast and available as the node. The rating parts of the pages also read the chain through Alchemy RPC on every view (each paper's requests on each of the environment's contracts): the contract reads made together go out as one Multicall3 call per round (three rounds for the papers list), within the free tier's 500 compute units a second; nothing is cached between requests (ADR 0035), so a server cache can cut those reads once traffic grows.
