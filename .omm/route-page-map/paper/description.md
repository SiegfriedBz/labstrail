/papers/[cid] (app/papers/[cid]/page.tsx): one paper by its PDF's CID, a 404 for an address that is no CID. Published, it leads with the title, authors and abstract, then the proof (submitter, signature, token, UAL, CID), then its Ratings section (#rating, scrolled into view when the page opens at it): one sentence with its latest score, phase 1's latest score or state, Rate this paper, or Rate again under a score (a requestPhase1 transaction from the wallet, then the rating page with ?new=1), every rating newest first, and the three phases. It is titled after the paper, canonical at its CID's canonical spelling, and holds a ScholarlyArticle, its breadcrumb and its latest rating's Review; until published it stays out of search results, and with nothing on the node and no run it is not found. While a publish runs (?event=), it shows the run's steps and polls the paper's status until it settles (state-transitions → paper-page), then asks the server to render again so the Ratings section appears. Crawlers that run no JavaScript get it whole.
