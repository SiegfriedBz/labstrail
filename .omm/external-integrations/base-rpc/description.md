Base Sepolia through CHAIN_RPC_URL, with viem: smart-contract wallet signatures are verified on chain (ERC-1271, ERC-6492), rating requests are read by the rating run and by the pages (a paper's requests, one rating; each call tried once within 5 s), the reconciler and the health check read each contract's pending set and requests, 10 at a time, the health check reads the oracle's and the publisher wallets' ETH and TRAC balances, and the oracle sends its transactions. Locally, rating runs, the reconciler and the rating pages use an Anvil chain instead (LOCAL_CHAIN_RPC_URL, ADR 0036), while the publisher wallets' balances are still read on Base Sepolia. The DKG node reaches Base through the RPC proxy.
