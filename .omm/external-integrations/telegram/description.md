Telegram Bot API (sendMessage): every message for the maintainer, in one chat for every environment, each prefixed with its environment and level (ADR 0038). createNotify (workflows/src/alerts/notify.ts) sends it with TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID, only logs without them (locally), and never throws. Activity: a paper published, a rating scored or cancelled. Alerts: a failed or cancelled run, an oracle without funds or at its fee cap, an orphan R-KA or one an oracle move may leave unrecorded, low wallet balances and old pending requests.
