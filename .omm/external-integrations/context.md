Settings for each service are declared per workspace and validated from one root env file (ADR 0004); secrets (the DKG token, the oracle key, the Gemini, Pinata and Upstash keys) never appear in messages or step outputs. Staging and production get their own contract, graph, Inngest environment and Upstash store; the Reown project, the pinning account and the DKG node are shared (ADR 0005).
